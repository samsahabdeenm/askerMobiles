PROPERTY KNOW-HOW

Questions before your next step.

Clear starting points for buyers, sellers, owners and tenants. Contact us for details about a specific property.

What types of properties can I enquire about?

You can enquire about residential plots, vacant land, agricultural or garden land, old or new houses, shops, commercial property, rentals and leases.

Which locations does Asker Land Promoters cover?

We aim to assist with property enquiries across Tamil Nadu, with a special focus on Tirunelveli, Thoothukudi, Tenkasi and Madurai. Contact us to discuss your exact town or area.

Can I contact you if I want to sell my land or house?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us with the property type, location and any details you can share so we can discuss the next steps.

Can I enquire about agricultural land or garden land?

Yes. Tell us the location and type of land you are interested in. Availability, title and land-use details must be confirmed for each property individually.

Do you help with house, shop and commercial property enquiries?

Yes. You can contact us about residential homes, shops, commercial spaces, rentals and lease options.

How do I get the price and availability of a property?

Send us a WhatsApp message or call with the property location or listing details. Price and availability need to be confirmed directly for each property.

Can I arrange a property visit?

Ask our team about arranging a visit for a property you are interested in. Visit timing and access depend on the specific property and owner.

What should I verify before buying land or a property?

Independently verify ownership and title, encumbrances, approved layout or planning permissions where applicable, land classification, access, boundaries, taxes and relevant records with qualified local professionals before committing.
